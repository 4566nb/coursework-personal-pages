Engineering education / Edge AI2026

An engineering study assistant
on Raspberry Pi 5

A team-built study platform using Qwen, course-material retrieval and a web-based question-and-quiz workflow for Electrical and Electronic Engineering.

My contribution

RAG development, experimental run tracking and documentation.

  • Qwen
  • RAG
  • Raspberry Pi 5
Approach & scope +

The wider team system combines local language-model inference with course references and inspectable answers. My work concentrates on RAG and recording the experimental process so individual runs can be traced and compared.

The co-authored study evaluates retrieval, calculator support and supervised fine-tuning, alongside separate Raspberry Pi quality and device-performance measurements. These are team results; my personal role is stated above.
