01 / ABOUT

An EEE foundation.
An AI direction.

I’m an Electrical and Electronic Engineering undergraduate at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China, expecting to graduate in 2027.

My interests sit where electronics meets computation. Working with circuits, sensors and embedded control has made me interested in AI tools that account for the practical constraints of the systems they run on.

Recently, I have focused on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) for a Raspberry Pi-based engineering study assistant using Qwen, alongside tracking and documenting experimental runs.

Wearable plantar sensing

PCB design and analysis of foot temperature and pressure data, exploring indicators for early diabetic foot monitoring.

  • PCB design
  • Python
  • Signal processing
Project details +

Summer research at Zhejiang University’s Ningbo Innovation Centre, June–September 2025. Work included sensor interfaces, analogue conditioning, data filtering, normalisation and feature extraction. This was prototype research, rather than a clinically validated diagnostic system.
